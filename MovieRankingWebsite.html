The Best Movies According to ME

My Top 3 Movies of All Time

One Piece

One Piece is an adventurous anime about Monkey D. Luffy and his pirate crew as they travel across the Grand Line in search of the legendary treasure known as the One Piece. It’s full of exciting battles, emotional moments, and unforgettable friendships. I enjoy it because of its inspiring themes, creative world-building, and how each character has their own unique dreams and story.

Johnny English

Johnny English is a comedy spy movie starring Rowan Atkinson as a clumsy but lovable secret agent. Even though he often makes ridiculous mistakes, he somehow manages to save the day. It's enjoyable because of its funny scenes, silly action moments, and Atkinson’s perfect comedic timing that keeps you laughing throughout.

A Thousand Words

A Thousand Words is a comedy-drama about a man named Jack McCall who discovers he only has 1,000 words left to speak before he dies. Each word he uses makes the leaves on a magical tree fall. It resonates with me
